01

NASA 70 is an interactive digital archive that gathers and visually presents the web projects created to mark NASA’s seventieth anniversary.

02

The archive is explored through a physical metaphor: navigation filters such as categories, tags and individual projects exist as celestial bodies in a gravitational field, draggable and animated by a continuous physics.

03

The projects are organized into three macro-categories: Cosmos, Knowledge and Craft. Descending through the levels spawns new bodies from the position of the previous one, like an orbital trajectory.

04

It speaks to designers, students and enthusiasts who want to inhabit the archive: to drag, to throw, to collide, and only at the very end, to settle on a single project. Or would you like to send us your project?
